How to Buy

Four steps. Takes about two minutes if your wallet's ready.

Get a Wallet

Download MetaMask or any Ethereum-compatible wallet. It's free and takes five minutes to set up.

Add ETH

Buy Ethereum on Coinbase, Kraken, or wherever you prefer, then send it to your wallet address.

Go to Uniswap

Open uniswap.org, connect your wallet, and switch to Ethereum mainnet. Paste the Optimus OG contract address into the search bar.

Swap & Confirm

Enter how much ETH you want to spend, set slippage to around 5–12% (meme coins move fast), approve the transaction, and you're in. Welcome to OG.
